Privacy Policy

How MG Analytics Center processes authentication information, connected-account analytics and related data.

MG Analytics CenterEffective date: 14 November 2026Language: English

This Privacy Policy describes how MG Analytics Center (the "Service"), operated under the MG Dijital Çözümler name ("we", "us"), handles information when an authorized user accesses the analytics dashboard or connects an external account. Contact: info@mgdijitalcozumler.com.

Integration status: Website reporting and Instagram analytics functionality are being developed and tested. TikTok account connection is planned and is not represented here as currently active. Data is accessed only where an integration has been enabled and proper authorization obtained.

1. Information we process

  • Account and sign-in information: identity and authorization details provided by login services for access control, such as a verified email address or provider identifier.
  • Connected account details: account or profile identifiers, usernames, display names, and other fields permitted by a connected platform.
  • Analytics and content metadata: published content identifiers, dates, links and captions, views, reach, engagement statistics, website visits, and search performance where supported and authorized.
  • Authorization credentials: access tokens and, where issued, refresh tokens required for authorized API connections.
  • Operational records: synchronization times, success/failure states, diagnostic events and limited technical logs needed to operate and troubleshoot the Service.

2. How we collect the information

Information may come from you when you sign in, from third-party APIs after an authorized connection, or from the Service's operation and database. For a future TikTok integration, the Service intends to use TikTok Login Kit and Display API only after permission is granted and the functionality is enabled.

3. Purposes and legal basis

We process relevant information to authenticate authorized users, retrieve and display analytics, store periodic measurements for trend comparisons, maintain service reliability, and respond to support or data requests. Depending on the context and applicable jurisdiction, processing may rely on consent, service-related necessity, compliance obligations, or legitimate interests where legally available. Where consent is legally required, we will obtain it before the relevant processing.

4. Data storage and security

Analytics data and some account-connection information may be stored in a PostgreSQL database managed for the Service. We use authentication and access restrictions intended to limit access to authorized persons. No method of storage or transmission is completely risk-free. We do not claim that every stored credential is encrypted at rest; credential storage protections are being improved.

5. Sharing and third-party providers

We do not sell connected account analytics data. Data may be exchanged with an authorized platform (such as Google, Meta/Instagram, or TikTok) to operate a requested integration and may be processed by infrastructure providers used to run the Service. Third-party services have their own terms and privacy policies. We may disclose information where legally required.

6. Retention

Analytics measurements and synchronization records may be retained to support historical reporting for as long as reasonably needed for those purposes, subject to applicable legal requirements and platform rules. Exact retention periods depend on the relevant dataset and are not represented here as automatically enforced deletion deadlines. Credentials should be removed or invalidated when no longer necessary for an authorized connection.

7. Choices, disconnection and deletion

You may revoke a connected platform's authorization using that platform's settings, where supported. Revoking permission generally prevents further authorized access but may not automatically delete historical reports already stored in the Service. To request access, correction, deletion, or information about stored data, email info@mgdijitalcozumler.com. Requests are reviewed and handled in accordance with applicable requirements. An automated self-service deletion feature is not currently promised.

8. Cookies and website analytics

Our website may use essential session cookies for login or service operation. Website analytics tracking is intended to operate according to the visitor's applicable consent choices. Additional details about the public website's cookie handling should be provided in its own live cookie/privacy notices where necessary.

9. International services

Using external platforms and infrastructure may involve information processing across countries. Any international transfer must be assessed and handled in accordance with applicable data protection requirements, including Türkiye's KVKK where relevant. This page does not itself establish that all transfer requirements have been met.

10. Changes and contact

We may revise this policy as the Service and integrations change. Updates will be posted here with a revised effective date.
